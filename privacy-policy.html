Privacy Policy

Restyle: Interior Design Shop · Last updated 5 October 2026

Restyle (“the app”) is operated by NGN Consulting Corp. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it, in plain language.

Restyle can redesign your room two ways: fully on your device, where your photo never leaves your phone, or using real AI cloud rendering. Cloud rendering runs one of two ways — through Restyle’s own AI service (paid for out of the credits or subscription you already have in the app, no setup required), or through your own Replicate account if you add an API key in Settings, an advanced option for users who prefer that. Everything below explains exactly what happens under each option.

2. How cloud rendering works

When cloud rendering isn’t available at all (for example, no network connection), Restyle falls back to generating redesigns entirely on your device using a built-in style engine — your room photo never leaves your phone in that mode.

Restyle’s own AI service (default)

By default, real AI redesigns run through Restyle’s own backend, which we operate and host with a third-party hosting provider. The photo for that specific design, your chosen style, any text you typed, and your anonymous installation identifier are sent to our backend, which generates the redesign (using Replicate, an AI hosting service, under Restyle’s own account — not yours) and returns the finished image to the app. This is paid for out of the free renders, credit packs, or subscription you already have in Restyle — there’s nothing extra to set up. Our backend does not store your photo: it’s held in memory only long enough to generate that one design, then discarded.

The first time the app contacts our backend, it also sends a one-time Google Play Integrity check — a token from Google Play services confirming the request comes from the genuine Restyle app installed from Google Play on a real device. Our backend passes it to Google to verify and uses the result only to decide whether the new installation receives the free starting renders, which stops people from claiming them over and over with fake installs. Google processes this check under its own Play Integrity terms; we do not store the token or anything Google returns about your device.

Your own Replicate account (advanced, optional)

If you’d rather use your own Replicate account instead, you can paste your own Replicate API token into Settings. Once a token is present, Restyle sends the photo for that specific design, your chosen style, and any text you typed directly to Replicate using your own account, bypassing our backend entirely. Remove the token in Settings at any time to go back to Restyle’s own AI service.

Your Replicate account, your data relationship. When you use your own token, you are subject to Replicate’s own privacy policy and terms for the images you send through it. We don’t control how Replicate or the underlying model provider it routes to retains that data — check Replicate’s policy if that matters to you.

To manage or cancel a subscription, or to review your purchase history, use Google Play’s own subscription management (Play Store app → Menu → Payments & subscriptions).

5. Security

Your design history and settings are stored in the app’s private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps by default. Any request Restyle makes — to our own backend, to Replicate for a cloud render, or to a retailer’s shop link — travels over HTTPS (encrypted in transit). No system is perfectly secure, but Restyle keeps its attack surface small on purpose: no account to compromise, and our own backend never stores your photos — it only ever holds one in memory for the moment it takes to generate that design.

6. Children

Restyle is not intended for anyone under 13, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app has no account system, we have no way to knowingly collect anything from a child in the first place.

7. International users

We operate from the United States. If you use cloud rendering, your photo is processed by our backend and/or Replicate’s infrastructure, which may be located outside your country. If you use Restyle from outside the US, be aware that data you choose to send off-device may be processed in the US or elsewhere, where data protection law may differ from your own.
